The Problem

Over-scheduled days are quietly costing students their sleep

Students juggling school, tuition, and homework routinely lose sleep and accumulate stress from over-scheduled, poorly-paced days. Most "smart" devices make this worse — adding more screens and notifications instead of solving the actual problem.

They also expose kids to constant radio-frequency emissions from BLE and WiFi radios that run all day, every day, on a wrist that never comes off.

Sleep is the first casualty

When the day runs long, bedtime is what gets cut — night after night.

More screens, not less

Typical "smart" devices add notifications instead of protecting focus and rest.

All-day radio exposure

The Solution

One system, two parts — built to protect rest by design

A radio-free ESP32-based smartwatch and a companion website that build a sleep-safe daily schedule, enforce healthy breaks, and give a weekly view of how well the routine is actually being followed.

Zero radio waves

No WiFi, no Bluetooth, no cellular. All syncing happens over a wired USB connection — built to reduce all-day EMF exposure for children.

Distraction-free by design

School mode locks the calculator and other non-essential apps during class hours — leaving only what a student actually needs.

Low-cost, repairable hardware

Built on an approachable ~₹1000 bill of materials — not a sealed, disposable gadget.

Features

Everything the routine needs, split across watch and web

The watch guides the day in real time. The website plans it, checks it, and reports on it.

Live timetable display

Shows the current period and what's coming next, pulled straight from the schedule set on the website.

Auto school mode

The calculator and other non-essential apps lock automatically during school hours.

Enforced breaks

After a continuous study stretch, the watch prompts a mandatory break the student must acknowledge before continuing.

Guided breathing mode

A simple animated breathing exercise for quick stress resets, especially useful before exams.

Wind-down mode

30–45 minutes before bedtime, the watch shifts to a calm, low-stimulation screen and locks distracting apps.

Stress-log button

A quick way to log a stressful moment, building a private record over time.

On-device compliance logging

Tracks breaks taken, breathing sessions completed, and wind-down adherence — stored locally until synced.

Schedule builder

Parents and students enter school, tuition, and home time blocks in a few clicks.

Sleep-window calculator

Automatically flags if the entered schedule doesn't leave enough sleep time for the student's age group.

Smart break insertion

Automatically adds breaks between long study sessions instead of letting them run back-to-back.

Weekly compliance dashboard

Shows break adherence, sleep-window adherence, and stress-log patterns over time.

AI-assisted revision planner

Generates a prioritized study plan before exams based on subject and weak topics — with one hard rule: it never suggests cutting into sleep.
